Some examples of ways you can use Litter Power objects.Some of the following examples will run only with Litter Pro, but we have provided Collectives for the Litter Starter Pack. Unfortunately, the version of Max/MSP that is current as I write (4.6) will not display Collectives in the Extras menu. That doesn't stop you from navigating to the folder Extras/Litter Power Extras/X-amples/ and opening the file directly...Contents========- Beatmania	Generate Dance Music beats with varying degrees of "intelligence"- "Counterpoint" Exercises:  White Counterpoint (*)  Pink Counterpoint (*)  Brown Counterpoint (*)  Black Counterpoint (*)  	A series of patches, all based around an identical algorithmic structure but using different random number generators for selecting pitch. This will hopefully put to rest the myth that "distribution doesn't matter." Distribution makes all the difference in the world.- Instable Oscillator	Your grandfather told you about synthesizers where the oscillators produced a different pitch every day of the week? Or every hour? Or every second? Here's your chance to experience 1960s analog instability- Phase Distortion with lp.scamp~	Phase distortion is a classic synthesis technique. 	- Poisson Rhythms (*)	Another exercise in rhythm generation. - Smoothing (Sensor) Data	An example of how to automatically track incoming data, while smoothing short term jitter and adjusting for long-term variation in levels. Thanks to Trond Lossius- Spectral Mutation	A wrapper around lp.frim~ handling the interface with pfft~. This example shows one way of controlling the explosive nature of certain mutation processes.- WaveScamper	An alternative approach to phase distortion ---X-amples marked with (*) are distributed as collectives in the Litter Starter Pack.
